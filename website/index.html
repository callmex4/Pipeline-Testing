Get Started

What level of hiker are you?

Knowing your hiking level helps you select routes that are exciting, safe and rewarding. Start with your fitness, previous trail experience and comfort in changing weather. Build your confidence one unforgettable summit at a time.

Hiking Essentials

Picking the right hiking gear!

The right gear makes a long trail safer and more comfortable. Choose supportive footwear, breathable layers, navigation tools and a backpack that fits properly. Always carry water, food and weather protection.

Where You Go Is The Key

Understand Your Map & Timing

Plan your route before stepping onto the trail. Study elevation, distance, water points and turnaround times. A thoughtful itinerary leaves enough room for weather changes, rest stops and breathtaking mountain views.
